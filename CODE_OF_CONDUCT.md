# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the RouteUI community a respectful, inclusive, and welcoming experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and different perspectives.
- Focus on improving the project.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Contact

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open a private discussion with the project maintainers.
